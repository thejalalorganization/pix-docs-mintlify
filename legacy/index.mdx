## Get started in three steps

Whether you're on a Mac, an iPhone, or a Linux workstation, you can go from zero to your first coding session in under five minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install Pix">
    Download the app for your platform — a signed `.dmg` for Apple Silicon, `.deb`/`.rpm`/`.pkg.tar.zst` packages for Linux, or the iOS build via TestFlight. See [Installation](/installation) for links and commands.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect an agent">
    Open Pix, navigate to Settings, and add your first coding agent. Pix surfaces capability-aware controls automatically so you only see options that agent supports.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start your first session">
    Create a project, open a new session, and type your first message. Pix streams live progress in real time — steer, pause, or hand off to another agent whenever you like.
  </Step>
</Steps>
